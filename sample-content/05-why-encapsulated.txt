Heading
  Sealed until the second you use it

Pull quote (display serif, one line)
  Every orb is a first pump.

Body
  PDRN is sensitive to air and light. In an open bottle it starts to lose its
  freshness from the first pump onward.

  So we hold it differently. Each orb is a sealed dose, kept whole in the
  bottle until you press it between your fingers. Nothing oxidises on a shelf,
  and nothing is exposed until it is already on its way to your skin.

  It is designed to give every application the same fresh dose as the first.

Stats
  30,000ppm  Hyaluronic PDRN
  One dose   Sealed per orb
  45g        About 8 weeks
