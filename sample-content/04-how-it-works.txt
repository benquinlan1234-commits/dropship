HOW IT WORKS
Section: how-it-works

Eyebrow
  How to use

Heading
  Three steps, morning and night

Subhead
  The orbs stay whole in the bottle. You break them open yourself.

Step 1
  Title  Tap 2-3 orbs onto your palm
  Text   Tip the bottle gently. The orbs stay whole until you press them.

Step 2
  Title  Press to burst them into serum
  Text   A light press is enough. The orbs collapse into a clear, watery serum.

Step 3
  Title  Pat into clean skin, morning and night
  Text   Press it in with your fingertips, then follow with moisturiser.
