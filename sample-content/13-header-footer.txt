HEADER AND FOOTER

Header
  Brand name   Orb            (Theme settings > Brand)
  Link 1       Shop   ->  /#bundle
  Link 2       FAQ    ->  /#faq
  Cart icon    always shown, opens the drawer

  If you set a navigation menu in the header section, it replaces both links.

Footer
  Tagline      Minimal skincare, made for sensitive routines.
  Column 1     Help    (a Shopify navigation menu)
  Column 2     Contact
               Questions? Email us and we will reply within one business day.
  Copyright    © {year} {brand}. All rights reserved.   (generated)

Cart drawer
  Title                 Your cart
  Empty                 Your cart is empty.
  Free shipping         You are {amount} away from free shipping.
  Free shipping reached You have earned free shipping.
  Upsell heading        Add to your order
  Checkout              Checkout

  Cart drawer strings live in locales/en.default.json, not in section settings,
  so they translate with the rest of the storefront.
