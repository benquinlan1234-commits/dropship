COPY RULES

Never use, anywhere in customer-facing copy:
  patented
  stem cell
  cure
  clinically proven
  treats
  any other medical or treatment claim

Use instead:
  may help
  supports
  designed to
  people notice / customers report

Why: these are cosmetic claims, not drug claims. Words like "treats" or
"clinically proven" change what regulators consider the product to be, and
they need substantiation you probably do not have on file.

Other conventions used throughout:
  - "Orbs", not "capsules" or "beads", for the product itself.
  - PDRN is described as salmon-derived. The product is not vegan, and the FAQ
    says so plainly rather than avoiding the question.
  - Results copy is hedged and paired with a visible disclaimer.
  - Prices, shipping windows and the guarantee are stated the same way every
    time: $39 / $69 / $99, 7-11 days, 30-day money-back guarantee.
