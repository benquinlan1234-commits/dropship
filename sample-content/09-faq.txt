Q  What is PDRN?
A  PDRN stands for polydeoxyribonucleotide, a fragment of DNA sourced from
   salmon. In skincare it is used as a hydrating, conditioning ingredient. Ours
   sits at 30,000ppm alongside hyaluronic acid, and it is designed to support
   skin that feels dry or looks uneven.

Q  How do I use it?
A  Tap 2-3 orbs onto your palm. Press to burst them into serum, then pat into
   clean skin morning and night, and follow with moisturiser.

   If your skin reacts easily, patch test on your inner arm for 24 hours first.

Q  Is it vegan?
A  No. PDRN is salmon-derived, so the serum is not vegan. It is not tested on
   animals.

Q  How long does shipping take?
A  Orders arrive in 7-11 days. You will get a tracking link by email as soon as
   your parcel leaves our warehouse.

Q  Which skin types is it for?
A  It is designed for all skin types, including sensitive skin. The texture is
   light and water-like, so oily and combination skin can use it without a
   heavy finish. If you react easily, patch test on your inner arm for 24 hours
   first.

Q  How long does one bottle last?
A  The 45g bottle holds about eight weeks of use at 2-3 orbs, morning and
   night. Most people order two bottles so they do not run out mid-routine.

Q  What is the guarantee?
A  Thirty days. If it is not working for your skin, email us within 30 days of
   delivery and we will refund you - even if the bottle is open.
